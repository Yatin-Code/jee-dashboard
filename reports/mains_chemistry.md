## 6. Actionable Strategy — 7 Chapters to Master First

Target the highest-weightage chapters that cover all three branches:

| Priority | Chapter | Branch | Weight | Why |
|----------|---------|--------|--------|-----|
| **1** | **Co-ordination Compounds** | Inorganic | 8.42% | #1 by question count; focus on Crystal Field Theory, isomerism, and magnetic properties |
| **2** | **Organic Compounds containing Oxygen** | Organic | 7.58% | Alcohols, Phenols, Ethers, Aldehydes, Ketones, Carboxylic Acids — vast topic with predictable reactions |
| **3** | **Hydrocarbons** | Organic | 6.32% | High-weight Class 11 topic; alkanes, alkenes, alkynes, and aromatic chemistry |
| **4** | **Redox Reaction and Electrochemistry** | Physical | 5.68% | Covers both Class 11 (redox basics) and Class 12 (electrochemistry, Nernst, cells) |
| **5** | **Atomic Structure** | Physical | 5.47% | High-repeat topic (Line Spectrum of Hydrogen appears in most sessions); numerical-friendly |
| **6** | **Equilibrium** | Physical | 5.47% | Chemical + Ionic Equilibrium; mix of theory and calculations (Kₐ, Kₚ, pH, buffer) |
| **7** | **Chemical Bonding and Molecular Structure** | Inorganic | 5.05% | VSEPR, hybridization, MOT — core Inorganic with high recall value |

### If You Have Less Time (5-Chapter Shortlist)

Drop #6 (Equilibrium) and #7 (Chemical Bonding) → master the **top 5** (Coord. Compounds → Oxygen Organics → Hydrocarbons → Redox/Electrochem → Atomic Structure) for **~33% of the paper**.

### Supplementary Advice

- **Most-repeated topics to drill**: Stoichiometry problems, Line Spectrum of Hydrogen, Crystal Field Splitting, Stereoisomerism, K₂Cr₂O₇ reactions, Carius Method.
- **Practical Chemistry** (7 qs, 1.47%) is low-weight but easy marks — know common tests (Lassaigne's, flame tests, confirmatory tests for functional groups).
- **Halogen-containing organics** (9 qs) overlap heavily with Oxygen compound reactions — study them together.
