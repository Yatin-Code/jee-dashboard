## 2. Class 11 vs Class 12 Split

| Class | Categories | Estimated Weightage |
|-------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Class 11** | Mechanics (38%) + Thermal Physics (12%) + Units/Measurement (6%) | **~56%** |
| **Class 12** | Electricity & Magnetism (18%) + Modern Physics (12%) + Optics (9%) + EM Waves (3%) | **~42%** |

> Class 11 material holds a **slight edge (56%)** over Class 12 (42%) in Advanced — the reverse of Mains where Class 12 typically dominates.

### Detailed breakdown of chapters within each category:

**Class 11 chapters feeding Advanced:**
- Mechanics (38%): Kinematics, Laws of Motion, Work–Energy–Power, Rotational Motion, Gravitation, Properties of Solids & Liquids, Oscillations & Waves
- Thermal Physics (12%): Kinetic Theory of Gases, Thermodynamics
- Units & Measurement (3% × 2 ≈ 6% combined)

**Class 12 chapters feeding Advanced:**
- Electricity & Magnetism (18%): Electrostatics, Current Electricity, Magnetic Effects, EMI & AC
- Modern Physics (12%): Dual Nature of Radiation, Atoms & Nuclei, Electronic Devices
- Optics (9%): Ray Optics + Wave Optics
- Electromagnetic Waves (3%)

## 3. Key Differences from JEE Mains

| Aspect | JEE Mains | JEE Advanced | Delta |
|--------|-----------|--------------|-------|
| **Mechanics** | ~25–30% (spread across 6+ chapters) | **38%** (single bucket) | **Advanced boosts Mechanics by ~10%** |
| **Optics** | **11.79%** (single highest chapter) | **9%** | Mains asks more Optics |
| **E&M** | ~24.6% (4 chapters combined) | **18%** | Mains is heavier on E&M |
| **Modern Physics** | ~12.6% | **12%** | Roughly equal |
| **Thermal Physics** | ~7.6% (KTG + Thermodynamics) | **12%** | **Advanced asks notably more Thermal** |
| **Experimental/Measurement** | ~2.5% | ~6% | Advanced values this more |
| **Electromagnetic Waves** | ~3.6% | **3%** | Similar |

### What matters MORE for Advanced vs Mains:
1. **Mechanics — especially Rotational Motion, Gravitation, and Fluid Mechanics problems** (conceptual + multi-step)
2. **Thermal Physics** — nearly 60% higher weightage than in Mains
3. **Units, Dimensions & Experiments** — double the weightage
4. **Numerical/Integer-type problems** — Advanced has *no MCQs*; only Numerical Answer Type (NAT) and Match-the-Column

### What matters LESS for Advanced vs Mains:
1. **Optics** — still important (9%), but no longer the #1 chapter
2. **Electrostatic numerical grind** — fewer formula-plug problems; more conceptual reasoning
3. **Electronic Devices** — less trivial Logic-Gate based questions; deeper circuit analysis

## 4. Actionable Strategy for an 11th Grader (Targeting JEE Advanced 2028)

### Phase 1: Class 11 (Now — Mar 2027) — Build the Foundation

**Focus in order of priority:**
1. **Mechanics (38% of Advanced)** — This is your single biggest ROI. Master:
   - Kinematics + Newton's Laws + Friction
   - Work–Energy–Power & Centre of Mass
   - **Rotational Motion** (Advanced loves rolling + angular momentum problems)
   - **Gravitation** (orbital mechanics, escape velocity derivations)
   - **Fluid Mechanics & Elasticity** (Properties of Solids & Liquids)
   - **Simple Harmonic Motion & Waves** (Waves + oscillations)

2. **Thermal Physics (12%)** — Go beyond Mains level:
   - Kinetic Theory of Gases (speed distributions, mean free path)
   - Thermodynamics (cyclic processes, efficiency, entropy concepts)
   - Calorimetry & Thermal Expansion

3. **Experiments & Measurement (6%)** — Don't skip:
   - Vernier, screw gauge, error analysis, significant figures
   - Often 1–2 direct questions

**Target for Class 11:** Cover 55% of the Advanced syllabus and achieve 80%+ accuracy on Mechanics.

### Phase 2: Class 12 (Apr 2027 — Nov 2027) — Learn & Integrate

4. **Electricity & Magnetism (18%)** — Sequential mastery:
   - Electrostatics → Capacitors → Current Electricity
   - Magnetic Effects → EMI → AC Circuits
   - **Must solve IIT JEE archive problems** — Advanced questions here are famously twisted

5. **Modern Physics (12%)** — High-reward, compact syllabus:
   - Photoelectric effect, Bohr model, nuclear decay, semiconductors
   - Can be completed in ~3 weeks; aim for 100% accuracy here

6. **Optics (9%)** — Ray + Wave:
   - Geometric optics (lens/prism combinations, microscope/telescope)
   - Wave optics (YDSE, diffraction, polarisation)

### Phase 3: Final Revision (Dec 2027 — May 2028)

| Month | Focus |
|-------|-------|
| Dec 2027 | Mechanics marathon — solve 100 Advanced-level problems |
| Jan 2028 | E&M marathon + Thermal Physics |
| Feb 2028 | Modern + Optics + EM Waves + Units |
| Mar 2028 | Full-length Advanced papers (2020–2027) — 1 per week |
| Apr 2028 | 2 papers/week + weak-topic revision |
| May 2028 | Mock tests + formula revision |

### Key Tactical Tips

| Strategy | Why |
|----------|-----|
| **No MCQs in Advanced** | Practice only Numerical Answer Type (NAT) and Match-the-Column. Build speed with decimal/fractional answers. |
| **Mechanics before everything** | 38% weightage + foundational for E&M (EMI uses rotational analogies, SHM appears in AC circuits). |
| **Formula derivations, not memorisation** | Advanced questions often require multi-step derivations. Understand *why* formulas work. |
| **Solve IIT JEE Archives (2015–2027)** | The single best resource. Topic-wise and year-wise. |
| **Negative marking is brutal** | Advanced has negative marks for *some* question types. Attempt strategically. |
| **Use H.C. Verma + I.E. Irodov** | Verma for concept building, Irodov for problem-solving speed. |
| **Join a test series** | Physics Galaxy, Allen, or FIITJEE AITS for Advanced-level exposure. |

> **"Advanced Physics is Mechanics-first, concept-deep, and calculation-heavy."**
>
> - **Top 3:** Mechanics (38%) → E&M (18%) → Modern/Thermal (12% each)
> - **Class 11 is 56% of the paper** — don't neglect it after board exams
> - **Compared to Mains:** Advanced asks more Mechanics & Thermal, less Optics & E&M
> - **Best ROI:** Master Rotational Motion + Fluids + Thermodynamics at a deep level
