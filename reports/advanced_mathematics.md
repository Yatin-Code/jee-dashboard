## 3. Key Differences from JEE Mains

| Dimension | JEE Mains (Careers360) | JEE Advanced (Vedantu) |
|-----------|----------------------|------------------------|
| **Calculus** | ~22.7% (incl. Diff Eqns) | **27%** (no Diff Eqns listed) |
| **Algebra** | ~44.4% | **40%** |
| **Coordinate Geometry** | ~26.7% | **20%** |
| **Trigonometry** | ~6.1% | **12%** |

### Specific Differences
1. **Integral Calculus weight doubles** — 19% in Advanced vs ~11% in Mains. Advanced demands deeper application (area, volume, DE connections).
2. **Probability is much heavier** in Advanced (14% vs ~3-4% in Mains). Expect multi-step conditional probability and P&C-linked problems.
3. **Trigonometry matters more** in Advanced (12% vs 6%). Questions often mix trig with calculus (limits, differentiation, integration).
4. **Coordinate Geometry share shrinks** (20% vs 27%) — Advanced focuses on quality over quantity; fewer but tougher conics problems.
5. **Differential Equations** appears in Mains as a separate 4.2% chapter but is absent from the Advanced data — possibly absorbed into Integral Calculus.
6. **Statistics** is a tiny standalone topic in Advanced (3%) but bundled with Probability in Mains (6.7% combined).
7. **Sequences & Series** drops from 7.4% (Mains) to 3% (Advanced) — still testable but lower priority.
8. **Vectors & 3D** (11%) in Advanced is comparable to the Mains combined total (~11.4%), but is counted as one unit in Advanced data.

## 4. Actionable Strategy for an 11th Grader (Target: JEE Advanced 2028)

### Phase 1 — Class 11: Build the Foundation (Now – Mar 2027)

| Priority | Chapters (Class 11 syllabus) | Why |
|----------|------------------------------|-----|
| **Tier 1** | Trigonometry (Class 11), Coordinate Geometry, Complex Numbers, Sequences & Series, Binomial Theorem, Statistics | These are the 11th-grade chapters that directly appear in Advanced; also foundation for 12th. |
| **Tier 2** | Differential Calculus basics (limits, continuity, differentiation) | Though this appears in the Advanced data as "Class 12" topic, start early — calculus needs long maturation. |
| **Tier 3** | Probability basics (set theory, P&C foundation) | Probability in Advanced is very advanced; strong P&C base from 11th is essential. |

**Goals for 11th:**
- Complete Trigonometry (11th portion) → 5% of Advanced paper, secures early marks
- Finish Complex Numbers → 9% of paper
- Complete Sequences & Series + Binomial Theorem + Statistics → 9% combined
- Cover Coordinate Geometry thoroughly → 9% of paper
- **Target: ~32% of Advanced syllabus covered by end of 11th.**

### Phase 2 — Class 12 (Apr 2027 – Dec 2027): Cover Heavy Chapters

| Priority | Chapter | Weightage | Approach |
|----------|---------|-----------|----------|
| **#1 Priority** | Integral Calculus | 19% | Daily practice of indefinite integration + area under curves. Most time-consuming chapter. |
| **#2 Priority** | Probability (Class 12) | 14% | Master Bayes theorem, probability distributions, and advanced P&C. |
| **#3 Priority** | Vectors & 3D Geometry | 11% | Relatively formula-driven; can be mastered with 2–3 weeks of dedicated practice. |
| **#4 Priority** | Differential Calculus (advanced) | 8% | Application of derivatives, curve sketching, LMVT, Rolle's theorem. |
| **#5 Priority** | Matrices & Determinants | 8% | Practice matrix operations, determinants properties, and system of equations. |
| **#6 Priority** | Trigonometry (Class 12) | 7% | Inverse trig functions, trig equations — connect with calculus. |

### Phase 3 — Revision & Test Series (Jan 2028 – May 2028)

- **Full-length Advanced mocks** — 2 per week, analysis on gap days.
- Focus on **Integral Calculus + Probability** in every mock (33% of paper combined).
- **Mixed-topic problems**: Trig + Calculus, Probability + P&C, Complex Numbers + Geometry.
- **Time management**: Advanced has 34 questions in 180 minutes (~5.3 min/question). Prioritize accuracy over speed initially.

### Key Principles for Advanced Mathematics

1. **Depth over breadth** — Advanced tests conceptual depth, not speed. Focus on understanding "why" behind every formula.
2. **Calculus is make-or-break** — Integral Calculus (19%) + Differential Calculus (8%) = 27%. Spend the most time here.
3. **Probability rewards patience** — 14% weight but conceptually dense. Start early and solve 10+ varieties of each problem type.
4. **Coordinate Geometry in Advanced is harder but fewer questions** — practice JEE Advanced specific conics problems (not just Mains level).
5. **Trigonometry is a force multiplier** — used across calculus, complex numbers, and coordinate geometry; ensure complete mastery.
6. **PYQ analysis is non-negotiable** — solve ALL Advanced Math PYQs (2020–2027) chapter-wise at least twice.
